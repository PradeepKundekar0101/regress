# Adopt.ai Help Centre - knowledge base v1

[kb-01] UPI daily limit
UPI payments are limited to Rs 1,00,000 per day and 20 transactions per day across all linked bank accounts.
New UPI users are limited to Rs 5,000 in the first 24 hours after registration.

[kb-02] Failed UPI payment but money debited
If a UPI payment fails but money is debited, the amount is auto-reversed to the source account within 48 hours.
If it is not reversed after 48 hours, the customer can raise a request under Help > Payments > Failed payment.

[kb-03] Bank transfer (IMPS/NEFT) timings
IMPS transfers are instant and available 24x7.
NEFT transfers are settled in half-hourly batches, 24x7, and usually arrive within 2 hours.

[kb-04] Transfer fees
UPI and NEFT transfers are free.
IMPS transfers above Rs 25,000 cost Rs 5 plus GST per transfer.

[kb-05] Adopt Card annual fee
The Adopt Card has an annual fee of Rs 499 plus GST.
The fee is waived if the customer spends Rs 1,00,000 or more in the card year.

[kb-06] Card replacement
A damaged card can be replaced from Cards > Manage > Replace card for a fee of Rs 199.
The new card arrives in 5 to 7 working days.

[kb-07] International usage and forex markup
The Adopt Card works internationally once International usage is switched on in Cards > Controls.
International transactions carry a forex markup of 1.5 percent.

[kb-08] Card controls
Customers can freeze and unfreeze their card instantly in Cards > Controls.
Online, contactless and ATM usage can each be switched on or off separately.

[kb-09] Merchant refunds
Refunds from merchants are credited back to the original payment method.
Card refunds take 5 to 7 working days; UPI refunds take 1 to 3 working days.

[kb-10] Unauthorised transactions and fraud
If a customer reports a transaction they did not make, the card must be frozen immediately and the case handed to the Fraud team.
Customers who report within 3 working days have zero liability.

[kb-11] Lost or stolen card
A lost or stolen card should be blocked immediately from Cards > Controls > Block card.
Blocking is permanent and a replacement card is issued free of charge.

[kb-12] Disputes
A customer can dispute a card transaction within 30 days of the transaction date.
Disputes are reviewed by the Disputes team and resolved within 45 days.

[kb-13] KYC documents
Full KYC requires a PAN card and an Aadhaar-based video KYC.
Video KYC is available from 9 AM to 9 PM, all days.

[kb-14] KYC rejected
If KYC is rejected, the customer receives the reason in the app.
A rejected KYC cannot be retried more than 3 times; after that it must be reviewed by the Compliance team.

[kb-15] Account locked
Accounts are locked after 5 wrong PIN attempts.
A locked account can only be unlocked by the Account Security team after identity verification.

[kb-16] Adopt Savings Pot interest
Money in a Savings Pot earns 7 percent per annum, calculated daily and credited monthly.
Customers can withdraw from a Savings Pot at any time without a penalty.

[kb-17] Fixed deposits
Adopt fixed deposits are offered with partner banks for tenures of 7 days to 5 years.
Premature withdrawal carries a penalty of 1 percent on the applicable interest rate.

[kb-18] Statements
Monthly statements are available in Profile > Statements and are emailed on the 3rd of every month.
Statements for the last 24 months can be downloaded as PDF.

[kb-19] Closing an account
An account can be closed from Profile > Settings > Close account once the balance is zero and there are no pending disputes.
Closure takes up to 7 working days.

[kb-20] Complaints and grievance
Complaints can be raised in Help > Raise a complaint.
If unresolved in 30 days, the customer can escalate to the Grievance Officer at grievance@adopt.example.
